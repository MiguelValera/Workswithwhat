## New category candidates

No ranking pages surfaced for any new category, so volume evidence is indirect and every candidate is low confidence.

1. **Smart blinds and curtain motors** (recommended). Evidence: Europe-wide retail presence of IKEA FYRTUR / PRAKTLYSING / KADRILJ, SwitchBot Curtain 3 and Blind Tilt, Aqara Curtain E1, plus Shelly shutter relays as a very common use (see the Shelly 2PM Gen3 page https://www.shelly.com/en/products/shop/shelly-2-pm-gen3); market-level only: https://gminsights.com/industry-analysis/europe-smart-home-market/market-analysis . Compatibility is a real question (Apple Home, Alexa, Matter). Fields: `motorType` (curtain-rail / roller / blind-tilt), `power` (battery / USB-C / solar / mains), `fit` (rail / tube / retrofit), `maxLoadKg` or `maxWidthM`. First picks (unconfirmed): IKEA FYRTUR, SwitchBot Curtain 3, Aqara Curtain E1, IKEA PRAKTLYSING, SwitchBot Roller Shade.
2. **Smart buttons and remotes** (recommended second). Strong Matter/Zigbee question ("does the IKEA button pair with Hue / Apple Home"), tiny schema: `buttons`, `power`, `controls` (what it triggers), `matterBridge`-style note. First picks (unconfirmed): IKEA RODRET and SOMRIG, Hue Dimmer Switch, Aqara Wireless Mini Switch, Shelly BLU Button1.
3. Smoke and CO alarms (Netatmo, Aqara, Tapo T110-class sensors): weaker "works with" question, ranked third. Smart speakers and displays: already partly covered by hubs (`hubType: speaker-display`); not recommended as a separate category.
