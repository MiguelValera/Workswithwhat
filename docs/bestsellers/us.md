First 5 (US): Govee RGBIC strip (H61xx), Philips Hue Lightstrip Plus, Govee Matter strip, Nanoleaf Essentials Lightstrip, Kasa KL430.
2. **Garage door controllers** (Meross, Chamberlain myQ, ratgdo, Tailwind). Apple Home and Alexa support is a common buying question (myQ's HomeKit stance). Fields: `garageControl` (opener / retrofit), `sensorIncluded`, `power`. First 5 (US): Meross MSG100, Chamberlain myQ-G0401, Tailwind iQ3, Genie Aladdin Connect, ratgdo (low confidence on all).
Also considered: smart speakers and displays (high volume, but partly covered under hubs), smoke and CO alarms (Nest Protect, First Alert Onelink), smart blinds (SwitchBot Curtain). Recommend 1 and 2.
