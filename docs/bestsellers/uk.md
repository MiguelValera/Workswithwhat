## New category candidates
Still judgement, not sales evidence (no volume pages surfaced). Ranked:
1. **Smart blinds and curtain motors** (SwitchBot Curtain, IKEA FYRTUR / Hue-free options): moderate UK volume; Apple Home and Matter are real buying questions. Fields: `motorType` (blind-motor / curtain-robot / roller), `power` (battery / solar / mains), `fit`, `hubRequired` (already common). Best sellers not found; collect from official UK pages.
2. **Smart speakers and displays**: biggest volume, but already covered by `hubType: speaker-display`; extend hubs instead (Echo Show 8, Echo Dot, Nest Mini, HomePod).
3. Smoke and CO alarms (Hive, Aico, Ei, X-Sense): UK demand is real but ecosystem is rarely the question. Not recommended.
Not fitting: air purifiers, mowers, irrigation.
Recommend: blinds only, and only if a second category is wanted now.
