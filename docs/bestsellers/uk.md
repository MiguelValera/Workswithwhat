## New category candidates
No sales data surfaced (searches returned only titles), so this is judgement, not evidence. Ranked:
1. **Smart speakers and displays as a category** (Echo, Nest, HomePod): the biggest volume in UK smart homes, and "does it work with X" is a real question. Partly covered by `hubType: speaker-display`; a separate category is not needed yet. Recommend extending the hub list instead.
2. **LED light strips** (Govee, Hue Lightstrip, Tapo L920): high Amazon UK volume, ecosystem and Matter are real questions, and the bulb schema nearly covers it (add `lengthM`, `colorType`). Cheap add. Recommended.
3. **Smoke and CO alarms** (Nest Protect discontinued; Hive, Aico, Ei, X-Sense): UK demand is real, but ecosystem compatibility is rarely the buying question. Not recommended now.
4. Smart blinds and curtain motors (SwitchBot Curtain, IKEA FYRTUR): moderate volume; Matter/Apple Home matters. Second-wave candidate.
Not fitting the site: air purifiers, lawn mowers, irrigation (low UK volume or ecosystem is not the question).
Recommend: (1) LED strips, (2) blinds if a second is wanted. Top-5 per market for these not found; collect from official UK pages.
